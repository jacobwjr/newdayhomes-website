Make it yours*

A factory-built home doesn't have to look like everyone else's. Here's what you can choose when we design your home together.

Layout

Move or remove walls, add a bedroom or office, enlarge the living room, or add a mudroom by the back door.

Kitchen

Cabinet styles and colors, countertops, an island, a walk-in pantry, and appliance packages.

Bathrooms

Tile walk-in showers, soaker tubs, double vanities, and upgraded fixtures.

Exterior

Siding and trim colors, roof pitch, shingles, window styles, and front or wraparound porches.

Finishes

Flooring, paint colors, ceiling heights, lighting, trim, and interior doors.

Comfort & efficiency

Upgraded insulation, energy-efficient windows, better HVAC, and smart-home wiring.

*Customizations are available within the limitations of each floor plan and manufacturer. Not every option is available on every plan, and some changes may affect price and build time. We'll walk you through exactly what's possible for the home you choose.

How your custom home comes together

Designing a home should be the fun part. We keep the rest simple and handle most of the details for you.

  1. 1
    Design consultation

    Sit down with us to talk about your budget, your land, and what you want in a home.

  2. 2
    Choose your plan and options

    Pick a floor plan, then choose your layout changes, finishes, and exterior, within the limits of that plan.

  3. 3
    Final price and financing

    Review your design and full price, then we help you line up financing.

  4. 4
    Built in the factory

    Your home is built to your specs while we handle permits, foundation, and utilities.

  5. 5
    Delivery, setup, move in

    We deliver, set up, and finish your home, then hand you the keys.
